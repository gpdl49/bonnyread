## Missed

Interactions the docs leave out or understate:

1. **Profilarr deletes media, not just config.** When its cleanup job is enabled, Profilarr also runs an "entity cleanup". It reads `GET /api/v3/health`, regex-parses TMDB/TVDB ids out of the `RemovedMovieCheck`/`RemovedSeriesCheck` health **message text**, and issues `DELETE movie/{id}` or `DELETE series/{id}`. See `profilarr/src/lib/server/sync/entityCleanup.ts:16-17,38-52` and `jobs/handlers/arrCleanup.ts:87-94`, and `clients/radarr.ts:180`, `clients/sonarr.ts:45`. This is the most destructive cross-app call in the set. It also matches on status text, which §1.2 #3 attributes only to Cleanuparr and Unpackerr.
2. **The Arrs push to Bazarr.** Bazarr exposes `webhooks/sonarr` and `webhooks/radarr` (`bazarr/bazarr/api/webhooks/sonarr.py:22`, `radarr.py:21`) and a Plex webhook (`plex.py:95`). The diagram shows only Bazarr→Arr. §4 mentions "webhooks" but §1 does not.
3. **Cleanuparr mirrors custom-format scores.** `CustomFormatScoreSyncer` (`Features/Jobs/CustomFormatScoreSyncer.cs`) stores per-movie and per-episode CF scores and their history (`EventsContext.cs:31-33`). A fourth tool therefore reads CF scoring from the Arrs, alongside Profilarr, Recyclarr and the Arr itself.
4. **Consumers of apps outside the 16.** Prowlarr syncs to LazyLibrarian and Mylar. autobrr and Cleanuparr support Sportarr (`autobrr/pkg/arr/sportarr`, `cleanuparr/.../Arr/SportarrClient.cs`). Cleanuparr integrates LazyLibrarian (`Features/LazyLibrarian/LazyLibrarianService.cs`). Unpackerr documents Sportarr as a Sonarr-compatible app. Profilarr has a Chaptarr client. These shape the "external contract" surface in §5.3.
5. **Auth styles differ per consumer:**
   - Seerr and Bazarr use `?apikey=`;
   - Bazarr's SignalR uses `?access_token=`;
   - Cleanuparr uses the `X-Api-Key` header.

   A monolith drops all of these, but any compatibility façade has to accept all three.
6. **Queue page-size handling differs per consumer:**
   - Seerr: implicit 10;
   - Profilarr: 200, no paging (`clients/radarr.ts:215`, `clients/sonarr.ts:256`);
   - Cleanuparr: 200, paged;
   - Unpackerr: 2,000 cap.

   Profilarr's single 200-item page is the same class of bug as Seerr's.
7. **Two Whisparr generations.** The `whisparr` submodule is v2 (Sonarr-based: Series/Episodes) and `whisparr-eros` is v3 (Radarr-based: Movies), both on `/api/v3`. The consumers handle both: autobrr (`whisparr.go:176-196`) and Cleanuparr (`WhisparrV2Client.cs`, `WhisparrV3Client.cs`). The §1.1 column "Whisparr (`/api/v3`) – same as Sonarr" hides that split.
